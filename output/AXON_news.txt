Title: Morgan Stanley says to snatch up these 24 AI-adopter stocks as their profit margins balloon
URL: https://www.businessinsider.com/ai-stocks-to-buy-now-companies-adopting-profit-margins-productivity-2026-8#article
Time Published: 2026-08-04T10:46:28Z
Full Content:
AI hyperscalers are under serious scrutiny from investors looking for clues about how they'll monetize their massive infrastructure investments, but they're not the only ones feeling the pressure. Investors also increasingly want companies downstream from AI to employ the technology to grow profit margins, Morgan Stanley said in a note on Monday. Given the heightened focus on efficiency and productivity, the bank published a list of 24 stocks that have AI adoption as part of their core to thesis and which the bank rates as "Overweight," or the equivalent of "Buy." "This group has seen relative net margins expand by 50 basis points in just three months, and its net margin profile now exceeds that of the broader market by nearly 400 basis points," Mike Wilson, Morgan Stanley's chief investment officer, said in the note. "In our view, it is no coincidence that this is occurring as the market becomes increasingly focused on earnings quality and margin expansion," he continued. "Attention is shifting toward operational efficiency, and AI adoption is a key driver." Here's the full list of AI adopter stocks that Morgan Stanley is bullish on.
